How It Works

Forward chaining is data-driven: start from known facts and keep firing rules until nothing new can be concluded.

Read sensors

Each reading becomes a fact such as door=open or time=night.

Match rules

A rule fires when all its premises are present in the fact set.

Chain conclusions

New facts, like mode=sleep, can trigger further rules such as intruder detection.

Resolve and act

Emergency actions override routine ones, then the final actions are listed.
